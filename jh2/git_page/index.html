About Me

I am a high school student with a curious mind and a love for connecting ideas across art, science, and community building. I created this space to share the projects and experiences that shape who I am becoming: a committed teammate, a thoughtful researcher, and an engaged citizen.

My interests include vocal performance with the Meistsingers, competitive cross country, local science initiatives, philosophical inquiry, and summer programs that stretch my leadership skills.

Each section of this site highlights the people, places, and lessons that have inspired me. I hope it offers a glimpse of why I care deeply about collaboration, perseverance, and curiosity.
